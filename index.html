Featured — Learno LMS

A classroom lesson player used by 100+ schools

Designed, built and deployed by me at Think Pro Academy.

Problem. Teachers in partner schools needed one reliable place to run 3D animated lessons, assembly instructions, slide decks and videos, on school laptops and projectors with patchy internet, without anyone having to be technical.

What I built.

  • A player for animated 3D lessons, interactive GLB assembly steps, PDFs, PowerPoint and video.
  • Role-based accounts (staff, school, demo) with per-school branding and per-account access to sessions, enforced in one gate so a new route can't forget the check.
  • Offline sessions, so a lesson downloaded once keeps working when the school's internet doesn't.
  • Plain-English activity logs written for the people reviewing usage, not for engineers.
  • A companion admin app for staff to publish content, plus parts-list (BOM) sheets teachers can tick off during builds.
  • A build pipeline with tests, production builds and automatic deploys, and a changelog and design notes that record the trade-offs.

A product decision I'd defend. Sign-in is by registered phone number on purpose: the goal is to know who uses the player, not to lock content away from staff. Less friction for teachers, real usage data for us.
