One idea for Vyapar: following up on unpaid bills

I only know what's public about Vyapar, so take this as a starting point for a conversation, not a plan.

QuestionWhat I think
What does Vyapar have already?Over 1 crore businesses use it for billing, stock and accounts. It can send bills and payment reminders on WhatsApp with a UPI link, billing works offline, it runs in 9+ languages, and Suvit (acquired Nov 2025) adds reconciliation and compliance.
What's the problem?Shops and distributors sell a lot on credit (udhaar). Sending one reminder is easy. My guess is the follow-up is where it breaks: owners don't want to keep chasing regular customers, so the reminder goes out once and then stops.
How would I check that?Spend a day each in around ten shops and distributors in Bangalore, especially at closing time. Look at how many reminders lead to payment within 7 days, and whether the UPI link, language or time of day changes that. Ask support and sales why owners turn reminders off.
What's the idea?Let Vyapar send the routine follow-ups on a schedule and bring the owner in only when a call is needed. Customers can reply with the date they'll pay, and Vyapar reminds them that day.
What would I leave out at first?Automated calls, anything that sounds like a legal notice, and late fees or interest, which are the owner's call. No new screens either; it should fit into the reminder flow that's already there.
How would it roll out?Start with owners who already send reminders, compare their collections with a similar group for a month, then expand.
